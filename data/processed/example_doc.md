## Services

Website design and development: Flowtron builds landing pages, portfolio and business websites, e-commerce stores and customer portals. Pricing depends on page count, design complexity, content, integrations and functionality. Indicative project fees range from USD 750 for a basic website to USD 40,000 or more for a complex enterprise portal.

Custom software development: Services include internal tools, dashboards, workflow platforms, customer portals and SaaS products. Scope, user roles, integrations, data complexity, security and testing determine the estimate. Projects may range from approximately USD 3,000 for focused tools to USD 100,000 or more for large enterprise systems.

AI solutions: Flowtron develops chatbots, RAG applications, AI agents, document-processing systems and enterprise AI solutions. Costs are influenced by data preparation, model selection, evaluation, integrations, security and hosting. Indicative fees range from USD 1,500 to USD 75,000 or more.

Business automation: Workflow services connect business applications and automate lead routing, approvals, notifications, reporting and data synchronization. Simple workflows may start around USD 500, while complex multi-system or enterprise automation can exceed USD 25,000.

CRM implementation and integrations: Work includes CRM configuration, data migration, custom modules, third-party integrations and sales or service automation. Full implementations typically range from USD 3,000 to USD 30,000 or more, depending on scale and customization.

Cloud infrastructure and deployment: Flowtron supports cloud setup, application deployment, containerization, CI/CD, monitoring, backups and security hardening. Implementation is quoted by architecture and complexity; ongoing management subscriptions may start around USD 99 per month.

Maintenance and support: Recurring services cover updates, bug fixes, backups, security checks and technical assistance. Indicative website maintenance ranges from USD 600 to USD 3,000 annually; software maintenance commonly starts around USD 3,000 annually.
