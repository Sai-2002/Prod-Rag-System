## Commercial positioning   
A hybrid model allows Flowtron to serve smaller customers through clearly bounded packages while offering established and enterprise customers a more consultative, tailored engagement. The objective is to make entry-level services accessible without applying small-project assumptions to complex or business-critical work.

Pricing reflects project complexity, integrations, engineering effort, security, scalability, infrastructure, support obligations, and delivery risk.

## 15.2 Profitability calculations   
Gross profit shows the amount remaining after direct delivery costs. Gross margin expresses that amount as a percentage of revenue, while markup expresses it as a percentage of cost. Management should use gross margin to compare projects consistently and should separately account for overhead, sales costs, taxes, and cash-flow timing.   
Gross Profit = Project Revenue − Direct Delivery Cost   
Gross Margin = (Gross Profit ÷ Project Revenue) × 100   
Markup = (Gross Profit ÷ Direct Delivery Cost) × 100   
Gross margin and markup are different metrics and should not be used interchangeably.
